## Dataset and labeling protocol

For each advertised PII category and semantic check category, reserve at least 50 positive
and 50 benign near-match development examples and at least 100 positive and 100 benign
near-match held-out examples. Evidence must include a stable safe example ID, category,
boundary, independent pass/violation label, label reviewer, source or template family,
dataset version, and a record of why the label applies. Keep raw examples in owner-controlled
storage; routine reports contain IDs and aggregate measurements only.

Split by source or template family before calibration so that paraphrases and near duplicates
cannot cross into the held-out set. At least one independent reviewer must verify each label
without seeing the SDK or evaluator decision. Resolve label disagreement before the held-out
run. The held-out labels and their versioned hash must be frozen before measuring the SDK.
Calibration may use development examples only. If a substantive change requires another
assessment, reserve a new held-out set and record the change; do not relabel the prior one
to make a failing gate pass.

## Gates fixed before assessment

| Category | Recall | False-positive rate | Classified coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Each PII category | ≥ 0.90 | ≤ 0.05 | ≥ 0.99 |
| Each semantic category | ≥ 0.90 | ≤ 0.10 | ≥ 0.95 |

All categories must pass individually. A run also reports denominators and confidence
intervals for each ratio, errors, skips, required-block counts, latency distribution,
available usage and cost, and sample counts and conditions for every supported integration.
Undefined ratios remain undefined. A failed gate blocks release.
